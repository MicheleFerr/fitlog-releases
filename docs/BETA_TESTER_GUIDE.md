# FitLog Pro - Beta Tester Guide

Welcome to the FitLog Pro beta! Your feedback is crucial to making this app great.

## What You're Testing

FitLog Pro is a fitness tracking app focused on:
- Workout logging with templates and custom exercises
- Progressive overload tracking (weights, reps, volume)
- Timer with background support and Live Activities (iOS)
- XP and tier progression system
- Cloud sync across devices

**Current version**: 1.0.0 (Beta)

## How to Report Bugs

### Quick Method (In-App)
Settings > Help & Info > **Send Feedback** — opens email with device info pre-filled.

### What to Include
1. **What happened** — describe the issue clearly
2. **Steps to reproduce** — how to trigger it
3. **Expected behavior** — what should have happened
4. **Screenshots/screen recordings** — if visual

### Severity Guide
- **Crash/data loss** — mention "CRITICAL" in subject
- **Feature broken** — mention "BUG" in subject
- **Minor visual issue** — mention "UI" in subject
- **Suggestion** — mention "IDEA" in subject

## Known Limitations

These are known and being worked on:
- Premium subscription features are not yet active (all features are unlocked during beta)
- Some analytics charts may show incomplete data for users with < 5 workouts
- Watch app connectivity may require manual sync on first launch
- AI Coach responses may occasionally be slow

## What to Focus On

### Priority Flows (Please Test These)
1. **First-time experience**: Sign up, create first workout, complete it
2. **Template workflow**: Create template, start workout from it, modify mid-workout
3. **Timer behavior**: Start rest timer, lock phone, verify it continues and sounds
4. **Offline mode**: Turn off WiFi, log a workout, reconnect, verify sync
5. **Data integrity**: Check that sets, reps, weights are saved correctly after closing and reopening

### Devices
Test on your primary device. If you have multiple devices, sync between them is valuable to test.

Your workout data is private and encrypted. During beta, crash reports and anonymous usage analytics help us fix bugs. You can delete your account and all data at any time from Settings.
