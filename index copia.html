Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 5, 2025

FitLog Pro ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Name and email address (via Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In)
  • Profile picture (optional, from your Google/Apple account)
  • User ID (automatically generated)

1.2 Fitness Data

To provide our core functionality, we collect:

  • Workout records (exercises, sets, reps, weight, duration)
  • Exercise templates and routines
  • Progress analytics and statistics
  • Rest timer preferences
  • App configuration settings

1.3 Device Information

We automatically collect certain information about your device:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system version
  • Unique device identifiers
  • App version
  • Language preferences

1.4 Usage Data

We collect information about how you use the app:

  • Features used and frequency of use
  • App crashes and performance data
  • In-app navigation patterns

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Provide Services: To deliver core app functionality, including workout tracking, analytics, and progress visualization
  • Account Management: To create and manage your account, authenticate users, and enable cloud sync
  • Personalization: To customize your experience based on your preferences and workout history
  • Analytics: To understand app usage patterns and improve our services
  • Communication: To send important updates about the app (with your consent)
  • Advertising: To display personalized ads (free tier only)
  • Security: To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security issues

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect your information:

3.1 Firebase (Google)

  • Firebase Authentication: For user sign-in and account management
  • Cloud Firestore: For data storage and synchronization
  • Firebase Analytics: For usage analytics and crash reporting
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: For push notifications

Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

3.2 Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the free version of the app. AdMob may collect:

  • Device advertising ID
  • IP address
  • User interactions with ads
  • General location (country-level)

Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Opt-out: https://adssettings.google.com

3.3 Google Sign-In

When you sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information (name, email, profile picture) from your Google account.

Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3.4 Apple Sign-In

When you sign in with Apple, we receive basic profile information or an anonymous identifier, depending on your choice.

Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Cloud Storage: Your workout data is securely stored in Google Cloud infrastructure (Firebase)
  • Local Storage: Sensitive data on your device is encrypted using platform-specific secure storage
  • Access Control: Only you can access your workout data via authenticated login
  • Regular Backups: Your data is automatically backed up to prevent loss
⚠️ Note: While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services:

  • Account Data: Retained until you delete your account
  • Workout Data: Retained until you delete specific workouts or your entire account
  • Analytics Data: Anonymized and aggregated data may be retained for statistical purposes
  • Device Data: Typically retained for 26 months (Google Analytics standard)

6. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format
  • Opt-Out: Opt-out of personalized advertising
  • Withdrawal: Withdraw consent for data processing

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the email below.

7. Children's Privacy

FitLog Pro is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. By using our app, you consent to the transfer of your information to the United States and other countries where our service providers operate.

9. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use the following technologies:

  • Local Storage: To save app preferences and cache data
  • Analytics SDKs: To track app usage and performance
  • Advertising IDs: To serve relevant ads (free tier only)

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting a notice in the app or sending an email notification. Your continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the app settings:

  1. Go to Settings
  2. Scroll to Account section
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm deletion

Note: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. All your workout data, progress, and settings will be permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.
