## 🎯 Strategic Ad Placement Analysis

### 1. Banner Ads (Non-Intrusive)
**Locations:**
- Home screen bottom (after action cards)
- Workout templates list (between items)
- Analytics screen top (above charts)
- Exercise search results (bottom)

**Expected Performance:**
- CTR: 1.2% (fitness app average)
- RPM: €1.20 (European market)
- User Impact: Minimal (positioned during natural pauses)

### 2. Interstitial Ads (Natural Breaks)
**Strategic Timing:**
- After workout completion (5+ minutes, 3+ exercises)
- When saving custom workout templates
- After extended analytics viewing
- During exercise selection flow

**Expected Performance:**
- CTR: 2.8% (full-screen format)
- RPM: €5.50 (high engagement)
- User Impact: Low (natural transition points)

### 3. Native Ads (Content Integration)
**Integration Points:**
- Workout template cards (every 4th item)
- Exercise search results (mimics exercise cards)
- Premium upgrade prompts (when template limit reached)

**Expected Performance:**
- CTR: 1.8% (blended content)
- RPM: €2.80 (good engagement)
- User Impact: Very Low (seamless integration)

- Unlimited private templates (vs 10 free)
- Advanced analytics and insights
- Priority support
- Cloud sync across devices

**Retention Strategy:**

- Usage analytics to show value
